EXPLORE CASE

Summary

As Lead UX, I led the digital transformation process for one of the most important international logistics companies in the region. The goal was to consolidate a fragmented digital experience and optimize operational coordination across multiple roles.

Strategic Leadership & Facilitation Focus

My work centered on creating a scalable Design System, redefining the information architecture, and implementing efficient handover processes, achieving a significant reduction in operational errors and development times.

The Challenge

International logistics is a world of high complexity where precision is vital. We encountered:

Operational Chaos

Critical functions uncoordinated and task duplication due to obsolete tools.

Cross-Platform Inconsistency

Web and Mobile App spoke different languages, confusing users and making maintenance difficult.

The main challenge was not only technical, but human: aligning teams with divergent visions toward a common goal.

Strategy & Process

We implemented a framework based on Lean UX and Continuous Discovery, ensuring every decision was data-driven and validated with real users.

Facilitation

Led 3 Design Thinking Workshops with more than 30 stakeholders to define the MVP and product roadmap.

Audit

Comprehensive heuristic analysis and product audit to identify technical and usability bottlenecks.

Roadmap

Clear stage definition: 1. Business Immersion, 2. Information Architecture, 3. Experience Alignment.

High-Impact Solutions

1. Scalable Design System

To resolve inconsistency, we built a modular design ecosystem serving both Web and Mobile.

  • +50 UI Components master components with atomic architecture.
  • +40 Variants covering all operational states.
  • Design Tokens (Color, Typography, Grid) to streamline implementation.

2. Information Architecture

We simplified dense logistics workflows into clear, task-oriented interfaces.

We mapped the complete Service Blueprint, connecting user actions with internal processes and support systems, eliminating redundant steps.

3. Process Definition (Handover)

I established a Design-Dev workflow methodology based on transparency and technical documentation.

We created integration guides and Handover manuals that eliminated friction in the implementation phase, ensuring the design vision was translated pixel by pixel.

Learnings & Leadership

  • 01

    Stakeholder Alignment

    The success of a Design System depends not only on the components, but on political consensus and adoption by area leaders.

  • 02

    Early Integration

    Involving developers from the discovery phase drastically reduces rework and fosters shared product ownership.

  • 03

    Design for Scale

    A token-based approach allows the product to grow and evolve without sacrificing visual consistency or technical performance.
