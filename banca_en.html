Project Overview

As Lead UX/UI, the main goal was to design a reusable, intuitive interface that would enable agents at a leading banking institution in Panama (Caja de Ahorros) to efficiently manage customers and digital channels, and strengthen its market positioning.

Lead Design · Reusable Interface · Customizable UI-Kit

The work involved leading the design by organizing projects, conducting key interviews across user profiles, and developing a customizable, brand-tokenized UI Kit.

The Current Problem

Banking agents in Panama spend most of their time trying to log into multiple systems at once to consolidate a single customer’s data. This fragmentation creates critical inefficiencies and endless waiting times.

Critical Initial Indicators

  • 17 min Approximate time per agent interaction.
  • +05 Systems accessed to solve one issue.
  • 03/10 Agent satisfaction with their tools.
  • 75% Difficulty onboarding new team members to the systems.

System Users

The product needed to consolidate operations for multiple customer-facing roles within the institution, each with specific needs.

Reception

Primary user at branch entry. Assigns tickets, reviews appointments, and routes customers into a queue.

Account Executive

Manages digital channels and serves customers. The goal was to centralize their tools.

Call Center Agent

Resolves issues instantly and remotely. Needs a consolidated view of digital channels.

Supervisor

Branch or team lead. Oversees key metrics such as inventory control and service status.

The Solution: A 360° Customer View

A way to keep all customer information at the executive’s fingertips to resolve issues instantly and proactively identify what we can truly offer.

Architecture & Integrated Selling

After a few alignment workshops, I worked on a functional architecture. The sales module would remain visible regardless of the customer’s process or issue, enabling “cross-selling” or “down-selling.”

  • 98% of agents feel systems need more visibility.
  • 98% need better, more accurate sales suggestions.
  • 8/10 customers come only to solve problems, overlooking business opportunities.

Centralized Customer Products

All relevant customer information consolidated in a single dashboard: Accounts, Cards (payments, statements, rates), Loans, and Investments—intuitively navigable in one place.

Before arriving at this final view, we worked intensively with paper mockups and activities to map a coherent User Flow before moving into final high-fidelity design.

Success Metrics

Data captured through surveys and system data after 6 months of implementation.

01

10%

Client

Thanks to the system architecture, the client was able to connect all their systems to integrate and resolve requests faster.

User

Executives reduced service time by 50%. The biggest benefit is cutting down cross-checks across multiple systems, increasing effectiveness.

Team

I developed a workflow so new designers can onboard faster.

Scale

Our product can be replicated in any bank and adapted to new needs.

Scalable UI Kit

Because the product would be reused across multiple clients and adapted to each one, our library needed to be tokenized and organized in a way that can be replicated without friction.

I aligned with the development team to create the central component library, atomically separating buttons, text fields, inputs, and navigation tabs that all projects can share.
