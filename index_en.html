Who am I?

Product Designer with more than 13 years creating people-centered digital solutions, leading cross-functional teams and scaling design processes in technology, banking, healthcare, education and E-commerce companies.

With strategic vision and facilitation skills, I have designed and optimized products that not only work, but inspire and generate business results.

My approach combines design, data, and leadership. I believe in building scalable Design Systems, in the power of continuous discovery and in the strength of a motivated team. I have formed and guided design chapters, aligning business objectives with user needs and contributing to the professional growth of other designers.
