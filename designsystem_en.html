EXPLORE CASE

Overview

The client, a promising fast-growing Fintech, needed a robust Design System that would not only align the brand, but also enable agile, structured expansion of its digital ecosystem.

Led a team of 7 designers

My mission was to lead a team of 7 designers, running 7 Design Thinking workshops and creating a system that integrated 3 sub-brands into a single library of 30 UI components and 40 variants.

The Challenge

With digital products already in production, the design lacked consistency, creating a fragmented user experience and making it difficult to scale the system.

Ecosystem Fragmentation

Digital products in production without a unified visual language, causing brand inconsistency and user confusion.

Compromised Scalability

Without a foundational system, each new product or feature required redesigning from scratch, increasing development cost and slowing the team down.

The central challenge: How do we design a flexible and robust system that supports the fintech’s growth and ambitions while integrating 3 sub-brands?

Strategy and Build

We mapped all stakeholders and ran 7 Design Thinking workshops to understand not only what they needed, but also their concerns about the concept of a design system.

System Principles

We defined clear principles inspired by top Design Systems: consistency, accessibility, and modularity. These became the compass for every design decision.

Style Guides

We developed detailed guidelines including typography, color palettes, and iconography, ensuring every visual element contributes to a cohesive and accessible experience.

Before/After Comparisons

We presented before-and-after comparisons to demonstrate the system’s value—showing how it solved existing problems and unlocked new possibilities.

High-Impact Solutions

1. Design System Foundations

We built a solid foundation inspired by the best Design Systems in the market, with clear principles guiding every decision.

  • 30 Master UI Components with modular architecture.
  • 40 Variants covering all states and interactions.
  • 3 Integrated Sub-brands within one coherent system.

2. Scrum Integration & Tools

We aligned the workflow with agile methodologies (Scrum), making the Design System an integral and continuous part of product development.

This allowed the system to evolve organically without requiring a team dedicated exclusively to its maintenance.

3. Documentation and Governance

We created detailed guidelines describing how to use the components and the underlying principles, ensuring consistent application across all products.

From design tokens to accessibility guidelines and Storybook, the documentation was designed so design and engineering teams could work in full sync.

Learnings and Leadership

  • 01

    Collaboration and Continuous Communication

    The most valuable lesson was that a successful Design System depends on consensus and adoption across all teams. Without smooth communication, the system won’t thrive.

  • 02

    The System Is a Living Organism

    A Design System isn’t a static project. It must evolve alongside the company and its products, so we planned for maintenance and continuous evolution from day one.

  • 03

    Simplicity and Consistency as the Foundation

    Benchmarking against the best systems in the market confirmed that success lies in simplicity. A complex system that nobody uses is worse than a simple one adopted by everyone.
