Case Study

MobiRep
Program

Transforming the medical visit with an innovative platform for automation and real-time management.

ROLE

Product Lead Designer

YEAR

2017

INDUSTRY

Pharmaceutical

KEY ACHIEVEMENT

90% reduction in physical materials

EXPLORE CASE

Summary

In an environment where digital solutions were in their early stages of adoption, MobiRep emerged as an innovative platform designed to revolutionize the medical visit process for pharmaceutical labs.

Product Designer & UX Strategist

I led this platform's development from scratch, designing a seamless experience on both iPads and the web, optimizing processes and providing a solution that saves time, resources, and enables data-driven decision making.

Key Problems

Understanding the core challenge for the sales team and management:

Lack of Digital Tools

The market was just adopting iPads; designing an intuitive and efficient interface for this platform was crucial.

Inefficient Management

Representatives wasted time carrying heavy, outdated materials to visits, reducing productivity.

Low Visibility for Managers

Limited information about visit performance, making key KPIs difficult to track.

Approach & Research

We implemented a Design Thinking process that included 5 key workshops, ensuring data-driven decisions from our 20 field interviews.

Rep Interviews

We conducted 20 field interviews. We discovered that 98% of representatives believed their work was delayed by not having the right updated materials during medical visits.

Managers' Vision

We focused on understanding what information was critical to them. A striking 99% felt they were not leveraging the generated data and that the marketing spending (paper) was poorly utilized.

High-Impact Solutions

1. iOS/iPad App for Representatives

We built an iPad app that allowed representatives to digitize all supporting materials, reducing physical load and enabling automatic content updates.

  • Digitization of product catalog and medical presentations.
  • Automatic capture of visit data (location, duration, doctor visited).
  • Minimalist interface that reduces required actions, letting the app work in the background.

2. Web Dashboard for Managers

We developed a real-time web control panel so managers could access key KPIs from each visit.

We simplified the interface so decision-making was fast, data-driven, and without depending on spreadsheets or manual reports.

3. UI/UX Optimization

For managers: a simple and clear interface adapted to their busy profile, showing only the most relevant data immediately.

For representatives: a fully digitized workflow that eliminates manual tasks and improves the quality of information captured on each visit.

Tangible Results

90%

Reduction in physical material usage by digitizing the medical visit catalog.

99%

Optimization in leveraging visit data for managerial decision-making.

$M+

Savings in marketing costs thanks to the elimination of printed materials.

Key Deliverables

User Research Report

20 field interviews synthesized into actionable insights.

iPad App (iOS)

Minimalist interface for medical representatives during visits.

Web Dashboard

Real-time KPI panel for laboratory managers.

5 Design Thinking Workshops

Facilitated as Product Owner to define the product roadmap.

Key Learnings

  • 01

    Listen and Understand Deeply

    Field interviews revealed key issues that hadn't been considered initially and became the foundation of our most valued solutions by users.

  • 02

    Automation as a Key Tool

    Reducing manual tasks not only optimized the workflow, but also improved the quality of captured information, generating a virtuous cycle of continuous improvement.

  • 03

    Adapting to New Technologies

    Working with iPads and HTML5 in 2017 was a technical challenge, but it allowed creating a richer and more dynamic experience that transformed the medical representatives' daily work.
