Scope Canvas: Understand the context

A diagnostic framework I use as a starting point to align Product Design work with business metrics—standardizing client context, goals, and expectations.

ROLE

Product Designer / Design Lead

PROJECT

Metrics for Designers

Goal

Establish a shared starting point: diagnose the client before talking about metrics, so Product Design can propose realistic, measurable strategies from day one.

Context

At the consultancy, we needed clients to clearly perceive how design impacts business metrics. Without an initial diagnosis, work started with assumptions and the conversation stayed around “deliverables”, not outcomes.

My contribution

I led research on best practices and synthesized learnings from previous projects. I designed and standardized Scope Canvas (v1.0), created onboarding templates for Product Designers, and piloted it in 3 projects to validate adoption.

Key decision

I defined that the starting point is not “measuring”, but diagnosing whether the client is ready to work with metrics—and which design strategy fits that maturity level.
